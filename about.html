Originally, I studied molecular biology at the University of Vienna. During my masters I got into computational biology and bioinformatics and found it so interesting that I moved from the lab bench to the keyboard. During my master's project at the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, I picked up a lot of coding and data analysis skills and found out that these skills are also sought after in the tech industry. Since I mainly enjoyed the process of coding and data analysis and wasn't keen on an academic career, I set my sights on the tech industry and have never looked back.

I went on to study data science at the University of Salzburg with a focus on computer science, statistics, and machine learning and includuing really cool collaborations with companies to apply machine learning in the real world. Most importantly, in the second semester, the folks from Blumatix, a small software consulting company turned startup, gave me a shot and hired me to work half time a as a data scientist to build ML models for the parsing of key details from invoices. Working in a small team for super smart folks tought me more than any degree ever could. Working and studying in parallel was a pretty busy time great for fast learning.
